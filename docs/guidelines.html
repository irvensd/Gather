Community guidelines

Gather · Updated November 2, 2026

These are the rules for posts and replies. Moderators use them when they review a report or remove something.

Disagree with ideas

Theological disagreement, hard questions, and criticism of church practice are allowed. Questioning Adventist doctrine is not a reason for removal.

Do not attack people

No harassment, threats, hate, or humiliation. Critique a claim without insulting the person who made it.

Protect private information

Do not post someone’s address, phone number, workplace, or other identifying details. Prayer requests should include only what the author chooses to share.

Be who you are

No impersonation of another person, congregation, or church leader. Use a public username. Your email is not shown on your profile.

No scams or spam

No fundraising schemes, malicious links, repetitive promotion, or deception.

If something breaks these rules

Use Report on the post or comment. A moderator of that community can review it. The author is not told that a report was filed.
