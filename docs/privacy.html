Privacy policy

Gather · Updated November 2, 2026

Gather is a community app for conversation. Irvens R Dupuy runs it. This page says what the app stores and who else handles it.

Reading without an account

You can read public posts without signing in. Gather does not ask you to create an account just to read.

Your account

If you create an account, Gather stores your email address and password, and the username you choose. A display name, a short bio, and a profile photo are optional. Your email is not shown on your profile.

What you post

Posts, replies, photos on a post, votes, and “I prayed” marks are stored so other people can read the conversation. Posts are public. Someone can read them without an account.

Saved posts and the communities you join are stored for your account. The saved list is shown only to you.

Reports and blocks

If you report a post or comment, Gather stores the reason and any note you add. Moderators of that community can see the report, including your username. The person who wrote the post is not told that it was reported, and the report notice does not name you.

If you block someone, Gather stores that choice so you stop seeing each other. The other person is not notified.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, Gather stores a token for that phone so a reply can reach you while the app is closed. You can turn notification types off in Settings. Signing out removes that phone’s token.

Scripture

When you open a passage, Gather asks a public Bible service for the World English Bible text of that passage. Your account is not sent with that request.

Support gifts

A gift is optional. The App Store or Play Store collects it. Gather does not receive your card number, and a gift does not change what you can do in the app.

What Gather does not do

Gather does not sell personal information and does not show ads. The app does not build an advertising profile of you.

Who else handles the data

The account, posts, photos, and notification token are stored with Supabase, a hosted database in the United States. Phone alerts are delivered by Expo. Scripture text is requested from the Bible service named above. Those services process the data only so Gather can run.

Deleting your account

In Settings, Delete account removes your sign-in, profile, avatar, votes, saved posts, community memberships, blocks, and notifications. Posts and comments you wrote stay in their threads so other people’s conversations remain intact, but your name is removed and they appear as written by a deleted user. This cannot be undone.

Children

Gather is not directed at children under 13.

Questions

Questions about this policy can be sent through the Gather repository. The community guidelines are a separate page.
